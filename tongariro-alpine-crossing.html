Tongariro Alpine Crossing

Bring home one of New Zealand's most iconic landscapes with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Tongariro Alpine Crossing. Spanning 20.2 km through the heart of Tongariro National Park, this world-renowned day hike takes you across an extraordinary volcanic plateau shaped by thousands of years of eruptions.

The model highlights the Crossing's unforgettable terrain, from the climb to the dramatic South Crater and the steep ascent to Red Crater, the highest point of the track at 1,886 metres. It also showcases the vibrant Emerald Lakes, the expansive Central Crater, and the rugged volcanic ridges that make this one of New Zealand's most unique landscapes.

Whether you've completed the Crossing, are planning the adventure, or simply admire New Zealand's volcanic scenery, this 3D printed terrain model offers a unique way to appreciate the remarkable topography from an entirely new perspective.

Carefully crafted to showcase every ridge, crater, and valley, this model makes a perfect keepsake, gift, or display piece for hikers, outdoor enthusiasts, and anyone inspired by the raw beauty of New Zealand's volcanic wilderness.
