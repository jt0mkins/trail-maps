Milford Track

Bring home a piece of New Zealand's most famous Great Walk with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Milford Track. Spanning 53.5 km through the heart of Fiordland National Park, this model captures the dramatic landscape that has made the track world-renowned.

From the tranquil shores of Lake Te Anau, the route winds through glacier-carved valleys and ancient rainforest before climbing to the spectacular Mackinnon Pass (1,154 m). The model highlights the striking elevation changes, steep alpine ridges, and deep valleys that define the Milford Track, allowing you to appreciate the terrain from an entirely new perspective.

Whether you've completed the walk, are planning your next adventure, or simply admire New Zealand's incredible landscapes, this 3D printed model is a unique way to celebrate one of the country's most iconic hiking trails.

Each model is carefully produced to showcase the natural topography of Fiordland, making it an ideal keepsake, gift, or display piece for trampers, outdoor enthusiasts, and lovers of New Zealand's wilderness.
