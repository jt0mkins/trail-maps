Heaphy Track

Celebrate one of New Zealand's most diverse hiking experiences with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Heaphy Track. Stretching 78.4 km through Kahurangi National Park, the track traverses an ever-changing landscape, from expansive tussock downs and ancient forests to rugged limestone formations and the wild West Coast.

The model highlights the Heaphy Track's remarkable terrain, showcasing broad alpine plateaus, deep river valleys, dense native forest, and the dramatic coastline where the track follows the Tasman Sea. Every ridge, valley, and contour is carefully represented, revealing the unique geography that makes the Heaphy one of New Zealand's most varied Great Walks.

Whether you've completed the journey on foot or mountain bike, are planning the adventure, or simply appreciate New Zealand's spectacular natural landscapes, this 3D printed terrain model offers a unique way to admire the topography from an entirely new perspective.

Carefully crafted to showcase the natural contours of the Heaphy Track, this model makes an ideal keepsake, gift, or display piece for hikers, cyclists, outdoor enthusiasts, and anyone inspired by New Zealand's remarkable wilderness.
