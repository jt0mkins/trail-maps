Rakiura Track

Bring home the untouched wilderness of Stewart Island / Rakiura with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Rakiura Track. Spanning 32 km through Rakiura National Park, this Great Walk offers a journey through one of New Zealand's most remote and pristine natural environments.

The model captures the track's distinctive landscape, from sheltered bays and rugged coastlines to rolling hills and dense podocarp forest. It showcases the gentle yet varied terrain that winds through untouched wilderness, where panoramic coastal views meet tranquil inlets and ancient native bush.

Whether you've explored the island, are planning the adventure, or simply admire New Zealand's wild southern landscapes, this 3D printed terrain model offers a unique way to appreciate the natural contours of Stewart Island from an entirely new perspective.

Carefully crafted to showcase the remarkable topography of the Rakiura Track, this model makes a perfect keepsake, gift, or display piece for hikers, nature lovers, and anyone inspired by New Zealand's remote wilderness.
