Routeburn Track

Celebrate one of New Zealand's most spectacular alpine adventures with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Routeburn Track. Stretching 32 km between Mount Aspiring National Park and Fiordland National Park, the track crosses the Southern Alps through some of the country's most breathtaking mountain scenery.

The model captures the Routeburn Track's defining features, from lush native forests and glacial valleys to soaring mountain ridges and expansive alpine basins. Highlights include the dramatic climb to Harris Saddle, the highest point on the track at 1,255 metres, along with the surrounding peaks, waterfalls, and the iconic alpine landscapes that make the Routeburn one of New Zealand's most celebrated Great Walks.

Whether you've completed the journey, dream of hiking it one day, or simply appreciate New Zealand's incredible mountain landscapes, this 3D printed terrain model is a unique way to relive the adventure and admire the remarkable topography from a whole new perspective.

Carefully crafted to showcase the natural contours of the Routeburn Track, this model makes a perfect keepsake, gift, or display piece for hikers, mountaineers, and anyone with a passion for New Zealand's wilderness.
