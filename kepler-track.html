Kepler Track

Bring home the breathtaking landscapes of the Kepler Track with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model. Covering 60 km through Fiordland National Park, the Kepler Track is a spectacular Great Walk that showcases alpine ridgelines, ancient beech forests, and glacier-carved valleys in a loop beginning and ending near Lake Te Anau.

The model captures the track's defining features, including the long alpine ridgeline between Luxmore Hut and Iris Burn, the climb to Mount Luxmore (1,472 metres), and the dramatic views across Lake Te Anau and the surrounding Fiordland mountains. Every ridge, valley, and basin is faithfully represented to showcase the remarkable topography of this iconic landscape.

Whether you've completed the circuit, are planning your next adventure, or simply admire New Zealand's rugged alpine scenery, this 3D printed terrain model offers a unique way to relive the experience and appreciate the landscape from an entirely new perspective.

Carefully crafted to showcase the natural contours of the Kepler Track, this model makes an ideal keepsake, gift, or display piece for trampers, outdoor enthusiasts, and anyone who loves New Zealand's extraordinary wilderness.
