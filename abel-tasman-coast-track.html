Abel Tasman Coast Track

Bring home the stunning coastline of New Zealand's smallest national park with this precision-crafted 3D terrain model of the Abel Tasman Coast Track. Stretching 60 km through Abel Tasman National Park, the track is renowned for its golden sand beaches, turquoise waters, and lush native forest.

The model showcases the track's distinctive coastal landscape, following a series of sheltered bays, forested headlands, tidal estuaries, and granite cliffs. Unlike New Zealand's alpine Great Walks, the Abel Tasman offers a unique perspective of the country's coastline, where rolling hills meet the Tasman Sea and every bend reveals another picturesque beach or secluded cove.

Whether you've explored the track on foot, by kayak, or dream of visiting one day, this 3D printed terrain model is a beautiful way to celebrate one of New Zealand's most iconic coastal adventures. The detailed topography highlights the natural contours that make the Abel Tasman a favourite destination for hikers and outdoor enthusiasts alike.

Carefully crafted to showcase the park's remarkable coastal terrain, this model makes an ideal keepsake, gift, or display piece for trampers, kayakers, beach lovers, and anyone inspired by New Zealand's spectacular natural landscapes.
