Great Walks Collection Set

Experience the incredible diversity of New Zealand's landscapes with the complete Great Walks Collection. This bundle brings together precision-crafted 3D terrain models of every Great Walk, allowing you to explore the country's most spectacular hiking and paddling destinations from an entirely new perspective.

From the alpine ridgelines of the Routeburn and Kepler Tracks, to the dramatic fjords of the Milford Track, the golden beaches of the Abel Tasman Coast Track, the volcanic landscapes of the Tongariro Alpine Crossing, and the remote wilderness of Rakiura, every model faithfully captures the natural topography that makes each destination unique. The collection also includes the diverse landscapes of the Heaphy Track, the rugged limestone country of the Paparoa Track, and the winding Whanganui Journey.

Whether you've completed every Great Walk, are working towards the challenge, or simply have a passion for New Zealand's remarkable landscapes, this collection is a unique way to celebrate the country's most iconic outdoor adventures. Display them individually or together to create a striking showcase of New Zealand's diverse terrain.

Carefully crafted to highlight the natural contours of each Great Walk, this complete collection makes the perfect centrepiece for any home or office, and an unforgettable gift for hikers, trampers, adventurers, and lovers of New Zealand's wilderness.
